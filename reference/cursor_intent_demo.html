Which answer is the cursor heading for?

Press Start, then move to your answer. A hidden Markov model watches each cursor movement and updates the chance you’re aiming at each option, using the forward algorithm in log space. After you click, Viterbi reconstructs your most likely sequence of intentions. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

This needs a mouse or trackpad: a touchscreen has no cursor movement before the tap.
