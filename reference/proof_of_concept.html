Welcome to the Lakeshore Daily Life Study

The Lakeshore Daily Life Study is an imaginary project about how people spend an ordinary day: where their time goes, who they talk to, and what makes a day feel good or hard.

We would ask adults from many backgrounds about routines, sleep, work, family, and the small things that shape a week. Every answer would help build a fuller picture of daily life in lakeside towns.

In a real study, this is where you would read about the research team, the funding, and why the project matters. Here it is placeholder text, so the page has something to read and scroll past.

When you’re ready, look through the questions below or go straight to Participate to choose how you would like to take part.

Before you start

This is a class project for BIOSTAT 615, not real research and not a real study. While you browse, this page records your scrolling, clicks, mouse movement and timing. It does not record anything you type, your name, or your email. Taking part is voluntary; you can close this tab at any time, and unfinished visits are thrown away.

In this demo, nothing leaves your browser. At the end you can save the recording as a file if you want.

This works best in a window at least 1024 × 650. Yours is smaller, so the page may feel cramped.

Please use a laptop or desktop
