Welcome to the Lakeshore Daily Life Study

The Lakeshore Daily Life Study is an imaginary project about how people spend an ordinary day: where their time goes, who they talk to, and what makes a day feel good or hard.

We would ask adults from many backgrounds about routines, sleep, work, family, and the small things that shape a week. Every answer would help build a fuller picture of daily life in lakeside towns.
